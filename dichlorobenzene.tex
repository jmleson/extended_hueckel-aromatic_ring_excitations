Symmetry behavior of the p-orbitals:
$$\Gamma_{red} = \,8 E\;\oplus{}\,-4 C2(y)\;\oplus{}\,-8 \sigma (xy)\;\oplus{}\,4 \sigma (yz)\;$$
$$\Gamma_{irred} = \,1 B2g\;\oplus{}\,3 B3g\;\oplus{}\,1 Au\;\oplus{}\,3 B1u\;$$


\begin{itemize}
\item SALC of B3g: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{4}}{2}$
\item SALC of B1u: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{1}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{4}}{2}$
\item SALC of B2g: \quad $\frac{p_{2}}{2} + \frac{p_{3}}{2} - \frac{p_{5}}{2} - \frac{p_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B3g: \quad $\frac{p_{2}}{2} - \frac{p_{3}}{2} - \frac{p_{5}}{2} + \frac{p_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of Au: \quad $\frac{p_{2}}{2} - \frac{p_{3}}{2} + \frac{p_{5}}{2} - \frac{p_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B1u: \quad $\frac{p_{2}}{2} + \frac{p_{3}}{2} + \frac{p_{5}}{2} + \frac{p_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B3g: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{7}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{8}}{2}$
\item SALC of B1u: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{7}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{8}}{2}$
\end{itemize}
Put together these SALCs form the following Hamilton matrices:
     $$ H_{B3g}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha & \sqrt{2} \beta & 0\\\sqrt{2} \beta & \alpha - \beta & 0\\0 & 0 & \alpha_{Cl}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{B1u}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha & \sqrt{2} \beta & 2 \beta_{Cl}\\\sqrt{2} \beta & \alpha + \beta & 0\\2 \beta_{Cl} & 0 & \alpha_{Cl}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{B2g}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha + \beta\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{Au}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha - \beta\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 


Solving Hückels secular equations, that follow from these H, leads to:
\begin{itemize}
\item orbital of B3g with energy = $\alpha + \beta$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\\\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\\0\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{2. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{3. SALC in B3g}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{4}}{2}\\\frac{p_{2}}{2} - \frac{p_{3}}{2} - \frac{p_{5}}{2} + \frac{p_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B2g with energy = $\alpha + \beta$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B2g}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{p_{2}}{2} + \frac{p_{3}}{2} - \frac{p_{5}}{2} - \frac{p_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B3g with energy = $\alpha_{Cl}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}0\\0\\1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{2. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{3. SALC in B3g}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{7}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{8}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of Au with energy = $\alpha - \beta$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in Au}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{p_{2}}{2} - \frac{p_{3}}{2} + \frac{p_{5}}{2} - \frac{p_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B3g with energy = $\alpha - 2 \beta$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}- \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\\\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\\0\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{2. SALC in B3g} \\ \text{3. SALC in B3g}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} p_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} p_{4}}{2}\\\frac{p_{2}}{2} - \frac{p_{3}}{2} - \frac{p_{5}}{2} + \frac{p_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\end{itemize}

Symmetry behavior of the s-orbitals:
$$\Gamma_{red} = \,8 E\;\oplus{}\,4 C2(y)\;\oplus{}\,8 \sigma (xy)\;\oplus{}\,4 \sigma (yz)\;$$
$$\Gamma_{irred} = \,3 Ag\;\oplus{}\,1 B1g\;\oplus{}\,3 B2u\;\oplus{}\,1 B3u\;$$


\begin{itemize}
\item SALC of Ag: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{1}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{4}}{2}$
\item SALC of B2u: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{4}}{2}$
\item SALC of Ag: \quad $\frac{s_{2}}{2} + \frac{s_{3}}{2} + \frac{s_{5}}{2} + \frac{s_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B1g: \quad $\frac{s_{2}}{2} - \frac{s_{3}}{2} + \frac{s_{5}}{2} - \frac{s_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B2u: \quad $\frac{s_{2}}{2} - \frac{s_{3}}{2} - \frac{s_{5}}{2} + \frac{s_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of B3u: \quad $\frac{s_{2}}{2} + \frac{s_{3}}{2} - \frac{s_{5}}{2} - \frac{s_{6}}{2}$
\item SALC of Ag: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{7}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{8}}{2}$
\item SALC of B2u: \quad $\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{7}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{8}}{2}$
\end{itemize}
Put together these SALCs form the following Hamilton matrices:
     $$ H_{Ag}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_{s} & \sqrt{2} \beta_{s} & 2 \beta_{s Cl}\\\sqrt{2} \beta_{s} & \alpha_{s} + \beta_{s} & 0\\2 \beta_{s Cl} & 0 & \alpha_{s Cl}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{B2u}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_{s} & \sqrt{2} \beta_{s} & 0\\\sqrt{2} \beta_{s} & \alpha_{s} - \beta_{s} & 0\\0 & 0 & \alpha_{s Cl}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{B1g}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_{s} - \beta_{s}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 
     $$ H_{B3u}= \left[\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_{s} + \beta_{s}\end{bmatrix}\right]$$ 


Solving Hückels secular equations, that follow from these H, leads to:
\begin{itemize}
\item orbital of B2u with energy = $\alpha_{s} + \beta_{s}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\\\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\\0\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{2. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{3. SALC in B2u}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{4}}{2}\\\frac{s_{2}}{2} - \frac{s_{3}}{2} - \frac{s_{5}}{2} + \frac{s_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B3u with energy = $\alpha_{s} + \beta_{s}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B3u}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{s_{2}}{2} + \frac{s_{3}}{2} - \frac{s_{5}}{2} - \frac{s_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B2u with energy = $\alpha_{s Cl}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}0\\0\\1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{2. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{3. SALC in B2u}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{7}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{8}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B1g with energy = $\alpha_{s} - \beta_{s}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}1\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B1g}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{s_{2}}{2} - \frac{s_{3}}{2} + \frac{s_{5}}{2} - \frac{s_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\item orbital of B2u with energy = $\alpha_{s} - 2 \beta_{s}$ \\
                            ( $\left[\begin{matrix}- \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\\\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\\0\end{matrix}\right] * \left[\begin{array}{c}\text{1. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{2. SALC in B2u} \\ \text{3. SALC in B2u}\end{array}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{2} s_{1}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2} s_{4}}{2}\\\frac{s_{2}}{2} - \frac{s_{3}}{2} - \frac{s_{5}}{2} + \frac{s_{6}}{2}\end{matrix}\right] $ )
\end{itemize}
